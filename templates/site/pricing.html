A few things, upfront.

Do I need a card to try it?

No. Levels 1–3 and every arcade game are available without an account or payment card.

What happens at level 4?

Create and verify a free account to access levels 4–6. Your trial progress can move into a new account, and future progress is saved across devices.

Are games included if I don't subscribe?

Always. Word Match and Word Shooter stay free. An account lets you save game progress; a membership is not required.

Can I jump straight to level 40?

Membership opens the full path, but you still pass each level's final test to unlock the next. The goal is learning, not skipping the adventure.
