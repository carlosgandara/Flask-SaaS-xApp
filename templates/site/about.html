WHY DESPACIO EXISTS

A smaller way to begin.

Despacio.ai started as a hobby project, born from the frustration of wanting to learn a language quickly enough to actually use it. Big lists and distant promises of fluency can make even starting feel overwhelming.

The idea here is smaller: learn something useful, try it, get it wrong, and try again. Order a drink. Ask for directions. Learn a few words through a game. Let those small moments add up.

“Despacio” means “slowly” in Spanish. That's the spirit: practical steps at your pace, with room to play. This is a growing project, not a promise that you'll become fluent overnight.
